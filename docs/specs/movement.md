# Movement

**Status:** built. Checked against the code on 2026-11-07. Climbing was removed on 2026-11-07.

**Values live in:** `src/shared/Movement/MoveConfig.luau` for the moves and the speed limits. Camera values are at the top of `src/client/Movement.client.luau`, the edge check's reach and headroom in `src/client/Ledge.luau`, and the cheat check's timings at the top of `src/server/MovementGuard.server.luau`. The grace times come from `src/shared/CardCore/Config.luau` (dash and push) and `src/server/Outpost/Outpost.server.luau` (travel).

## How it works

- Each player's client moves its own Humanoid. `src/client/Movement.client.luau` runs the pure rules in `src/shared/Movement/MoveState.luau` every frame. Roblox Server Authority was considered and declined.
- The camera is locked to first person (`StarterPlayer.CameraMode = LockFirstPerson`). Gravity is 75 (River lowered it from 150 on 2026-11-10; Roblox's own is 196.2), for a slow, floaty arc: jumps reach the same heights and take about 1.4 times as long as they did at 150. Both are set in `default.project.json`.
- The character turns with the camera every frame. The client sends its pose (Normal, Crouch or Slide) to the server, which stores it as the character's `MovePose` attribute. `src/client/RemotePoses.client.luau` draws every body. Soldier bodies ease toward their gun pose every frame, at full framerate. Other bodies use the poses in `src/shared/Movement/BodyPose.luau`: crouching (root joint lowered 1.5 studs, knees bent) and sliding (lowered 1.5 studs and tilted back 50 degrees). Bodies don't jitter: that stays on the first-person arms only. Only the root part collides with the world.
- Third-person jump and slide (2026-11-11, not seen in Play by Claude), in `RemotePoses.client.luau`. Jump: the going-up pose blends into the coming-down pose by vertical speed (`AIR_FULL`) where it used to flip at one speed; the knee that drives up is the leg that was swinging at takeoff (`airLead`, legs mirrored); the hips tip forward going up, back coming down and into the way of travel (`applyAir`); the arms swing out to the sides for balance, the right with the gun in its hand and the left off the gun, lifting more over the top and coming back onto the gun once the body is falling faster than 5 studs a second, so it lands in its shooting position (`AIR_ARMS_*`; not while shooting, reloading or with the cards out); and the landing crouch is mixed in as deep as the fall was fast (`LAND_FULL`, at least `LAND_LEAST`), hit in 0.05s and risen out of over 0.42s, where every landing used to hold the full crouch for 0.25s. Slide (`applySlide`): the lean-back whips past the pose going in and settles, the hips shudder with speed, and the left hand comes off the gun and drags its fingertips on the ground beside the hip (`ArmReach`), the torso tipped over it and the head level, with orange sparks and dust from the spot (`src/client/SlideSparks.luau`); no hand drag while reloading or with the cards out. `GunStance.pick` no longer chooses Land.
- Third-person cards (2026-11-11, not seen in Play by Claude): with a card out the body puts its gun away and plays first person's own deck flourish, then holds the deck; turning to the next card flips one across and using a card crushes it in the right fist, on the same timing as first person (`CardFlourish`, driven in `sampleStance` in `RemotePoses.client.luau` as `GunView.showCard` drives it). The client tells the server whether a card is out and how many have been turned and used (`MovementRemotes.Cards`, kept as the character's `CardsOut`, `CardTurns` and `CardUsed` attributes; your own are on the player at once). First person works the flourish out in front of the camera; on the body it hangs from a frame in front of the upper torso, facing the way the body faces, that tips with the look (`CARD_NECK`, `CARD_OUT`, `CARD_LEAN`; it hangs from the top of the torso and leans forward with it, so the cards stay in front of the chest when the run leans the body, but it doesn't take the torso's twist or tip), both arms are bent to its palms with `ArmReach` (`applyCards`), the fingers take its shapes (`BodyHands`), and the head faces exactly where the gun pose has it (it dropped to watch the cards at first; River had that taken out). The torso is stood up while the cards are out: 80% of the pose's forward lean is taken back out at the waist (`CARD_UPRIGHT`), since leaning in over a deck read as hunched. A jump with the cards out is kept quiet: the legs mostly hang as in the coming-down pose, the body tips less and the landing is shallower (`CARD_AIR_*`, `CARD_LAND_SHARE`), since the full jump under the flourish looked goofy. The deck is `src/client/BodyCards.luau`: 32 plain navy cards, the face-up one in bone, with no card art or bending.
- Third-person wall jump (2026-11-11, not seen in Play by Claude): the client tells the server each wall jump and which way is out of the wall (`MovementRemotes.WallJump`, kept as the character's `WallKick` and `WallKickAt` attributes; your own is set on the player at once). For 0.45s the body leans away from the wall, the leg nearer it shoves toward it straight, and the other leg tucks, over the jump pose (`applyWallKick` in `RemotePoses.client.luau`). The slide pose was left alone: River said it looks fine.
- Test view (2026-11-11, not tried in Play): P in the test room pulls the camera back behind you so you can watch your own soldier body (`src/client/ThirdPersonTest.client.luau`). Pressing P again swaps it to the front, looking back at you, and a third press goes back to first person (a separate O key for the front never worked for River and was dropped). The camera is pulled in when a wall is in its way, and is put back in its real place before each frame so the game never sees the test view. It is only a view laid over first person: shots still come from your head, so the crosshair doesn't mark where they land. P is now a reserved key.
- The run (2026-11-11, not seen in Play by Claude): River found the third-person legs wrong, since they flipped between four still poses. On the ground and moving (not sliding), a soldier's legs now come from `src/shared/Movement/LegCycle.luau`, worked out every frame in `applyRun` in `RemotePoses.client.luau`: each foot is planted for part of the stride and moves back under the body exactly as fast as the body covers ground, then lifts and swings forward; hip, knee and ankle angles are solved from where the foot should be. Stride length, time on the ground, foot lift and hip bob all change with speed (`LegCycle.shape`). The legs step the way the body is going, so strafing and backing up work. The hips' height and lean are read from the pose being shown so the feet stay down. While running the rest of the body holds one pose (`STEADY`) where it used to flip between contact and pass. The run eases in and out over the pose (`LEG_EASE`). Jump, fall, land, slide and standing still use the pose files as before. After River watched it: the swinging foot lifts about half as high (up to 0.65 studs, was 1.25), and each foot sits 0.18 outside its hip and can't come more than 0.12 inside it, so the legs stop clipping (`SPLAY`, `INSIDE`). Later the same day River saw the lower body leaning left and the run out of time with the step sounds. The hips now run square (the pose's turn and sideways tip are taken out while running, in `applyRun`). The feet play the step sounds as they land (`LegCycle.lands`, in `sampleStance`): your own in your ears, other players' from their feet; `Movement.client.luau` only plays steps for a body that isn't the soldier. Footfalls are `LegCycle.stepStuds(speed)` apart, about 6.5 studs at sprint, where the sounds used to be a flat 9; the first-person bob keeps the same spacing. `StrideStuds` is now only for non-soldier bodies. River then asked for smoother and more swagger: the swinging foot leaves and meets the ground still moving back (`CARRY`), its lift eases in and out, the stride reshapes over a few frames when the speed changes, and the hips sway over the planted foot, turn with the stride and drop on the swinging side while the shoulders turn the other way and the head stays ahead (`LegCycle.hips`, `SWAY` 0.16 studs, `TWIST` 11 degrees, `ROLL` 5 degrees, `SHOULDERS` in `RemotePoses`). The legs allow for the hips so planted feet stay put. Leg lengths and rest angles in `LegCycle` are the rig's, from `BodyPoses.Empty.rig`.
- Soldier bodies (the character model has the attribute `SoldierRig = true`) show gun poses from the pose files instead of the crouch and slide layers. `src/shared/Movement/BodyPoses/` holds one module per gun (made by `tools/build_body_poses.py` from `tools/export_body_poses.py`'s JSON; don't edit by hand) plus `Empty` for no gun, which also holds the soldier rig's joints (`rig`, root 3 studs above the feet). `src/shared/Movement/GunStance.luau` picks the pose each held frame: Reload (in a slide, a jump, a fall or a landing only the reload pose's arms are used and the body keeps its own pose, since 2026-11-11; `KEEPS_BODY` in `RemotePoses`), then Slide, Jump_Up or Fall in the air, Land for 0.25 seconds, Fire for 0.17 seconds after a shot, then a four-beat stride (contact, pass, then both with the legs mirrored) for crouch walk, walk and sprint (20 studs per second and up), else Stand or Crouch_Idle. Sidearms without poses use the Energy Pistol's. `src/client/HeldGuns.luau` puts the gun model in the hand, sized to the pose file's gun, and moves the magazine and charging handle or pump with the pose; your own held gun always hides in locked first person (the slide pose moves the head away from the camera, so distance alone wasn't enough). Bodies with no player, such as the soldier test dummy, are posed too: they carry the `PosedBody` tag and name their gun with a `GunId` attribute. A body's gun goes when the body is destroyed or leaves the game (a replaced body is only taken out, not destroyed). Third-person hands are jointed since 2026-11-11 (not seen in Play by Claude): `src/client/BodyHands.luau` gives each soldier body a copy of the first-person palms and finger segments (`GunModels.SoldierArms`), placed on the body's hand parts every frame with `SoldierPose.handParts`, and hides the body's own solid hands. The fingers hold the gun grip unless given another shape. The first-person hand and the body's hand part sit in the same place (measured in Play), so no fitting was needed.
- Only `src/client/Input.luau` reads keys, so touch and controller can be added later.
- Movement never changes accuracy. You can shoot in every move.

## Keys

These are the defaults. Players can rebind all but W, A, S and D in Settings (`outpost-ui.md`).

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W, A, S, D | Move (Roblox's default controls) |
| Left Shift or C | Crouch, slide (defaults since 2026-11-11; were C and Left Ctrl). Sprint has no key: moving is always sprinting, and its binding was taken out of settings. Saves still on the old default keys move to the new ones (`cleanKeys` in `Settings.luau`). |
| Space | Jump, edge parry |

Speeds are studs per second.

| Move | Rule |
| --- | --- |

Now a wall within 1.5 studs ahead (a blockcast of the root part) takes the into-the-wall part out of the goal, and if the velocity was set last frame and the body still isn't moving that way, nothing more is set until the keys change (`WALL_FEEL`, `STALL_SHARE`, `lastPush` in `Movement.client.luau`). Not tried in Play. On 2026-11-11 River had the air steering taken out ("perfect air strafing ... doesn't feel right"): in the air the game no longer sets the velocity toward the keys, `AirResponse` is gone, and only the Humanoid's own air control is left; the ground and takeoff parts below stay. Not tried in Play. Added 2026-11-10, not tried in Play. The move keys take hold at once: `onFrame` in `Movement.client.luau` sets the flat velocity itself, straight to the keys on the ground (and to a stop with none held) and quickly toward them in the air (`AirResponse`). Air with no keys held is left alone. River found the Humanoid's own easing felt like thick air. | Later the same day River still found it mushy and saw a stutter on slide boosts: the air now follows the keys faster (`AirResponse` 30, was 14), a jump's speed is there the moment you leave the ground, and a slide that loses the ground for under 0.3s (`SlideBlip`) carries on slowing instead of starting again at full speed, which had made the speed lurch. Not tried in Play, and the stutter's cause is a best guess. Roblox draws a character where its last 240-a-second physics step left it, so at frame rates 240 doesn't divide by, the first-person camera moves in uneven jumps. ## Camera

- Field of view is 80. It eases (rate 7) to 88 in a slide, 84 in a jump, 96 in a slide jump and 104 in a chain jump, and back to 80 on landing.
- Aiming the Longscope sets field of view 30, which overrides all of the above.
- The camera sits level with the soldier's eye bar, 0.94 above Roblox's default spot and 0.5 forward of the root, so it can't poke through walls (`BODY_CAMERA`).
- A slide drops the camera 2 studs.
- Landing after at least 0.15s in the air dips the camera 0.9 studs.

## Sounds

- A footstep plays every 9 studs while walking or sprinting, and a jump sound plays when a jump starts.
- Landing after at least 0.15s in the air plays a landing sound: the hard one if the fall reached 60 studs per second (a long drop), else the soft one. `HardLandSpeed` in `MoveConfig.luau`.
- Every slide plays the slide start sound as it begins, including one started by landing, so a landing into a slide plays both.
- `docs/sound-list.md` lists what's still missing, such as the slide loop and the parry.
- Roblox's default running sound is muted on every character.

## Cheat checks

The server checks every player's movement in `src/server/MovementGuard.server.luau`, using `src/shared/Movement/SpeedLimit.luau`.

- **One limit for every state.** This is deliberate: a client could fake its state, and slide jumps legally hold high speed. Horizontal movement may be at most 43 studs per second (the fastest chain jump plus a margin) times the time passed, plus 4 studs for lag. Rising may be at most 60 per second times the time passed, plus 4. Both are measured from the oldest sample in a 0.5s window, and one jump in position is credited with at most 1.3s.
- Falling is never checked. Hovering isn't detected.
- Checks run every 0.1s, with 1s of grace after spawning.
- Moves the server causes pause the check through `MoveGrace.give`: Phase Dash for 1s, an Aegis Shell push for 0.5s and Outpost travel for 1s, each plus twice the player's ping.
- A failed check moves the player back to their last valid position and adds a strike. Checks then pause for 0.25s, or twice the ping plus 0.1s if that's longer, so the move-back can reach the client.
- 5 strikes kick the player from the server with "Removed for unusual movement." and a server `warn`. There are no bans. Strikes survive respawns and clear when the player leaves. Once matches exist, strikes should remove the player from the match instead (see `matches.md`).
- The pose remote accepts only Normal, Crouch and Slide, at most 10 per second.
